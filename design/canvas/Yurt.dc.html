The stay

Three guest yurts a short walk from the water, with the family's herd on the pasture behind. Nights are cold even in July (often near 0 °C); the stove is lit for you at dusk. No road noise, no Wi-Fi, a lot of sky.

Getting there

No public transport. Road over Kalmak-Ashuu pass, open about mid-June to September.

  • Transfer from Kochkor with Bakyt · 5,000 KGS per vehicle, one way, up to 4 people, 3 h
  • Own car: 4×4 recommended. GPS pin sent after confirmation.

Payment & cancellation
