Overview

A classic loop from Karakol: up through spruce forest to camp below the pass, over the 3,860 m Ala-Kul pass to the turquoise lake, then down to the Altyn-Arashan hot springs. Long days and real altitude; worth it if you are fit and have hiked in mountains before.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Karakol → Sirota camp (3,100 m)

    07:30 pickup, 40 min drive to Ak-Suu checkpoint. 6 h walk, 1,100 m up through forest. Tent camp, dinner by the river.

  2. Day 2 · Over the pass (3,860 m) → Altyn-Arashan

    Early start. 2 h to Ala-Kul lake, steep scree to the pass, long descent. 8 h total. Night in tents beside the hot springs.

  3. Day 3 · Altyn-Arashan → Karakol

    Morning soak, 4 h down the valley, transfer back. In Karakol by about 16:00.

Before you book

  • Fitness: 6–8 h walking a day, 1,200 m ascent on Day 2.
  • Altitude: overnight at 3,100 m; pass at 3,860 m. Spend a night in Karakol (1,700 m) first.
  • Age: 14+; under 18 with an adult.
  • Bring: broken-in boots, rain jacket, warm layer, headlamp, 30–40 L pack.
